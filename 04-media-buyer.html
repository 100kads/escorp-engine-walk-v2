Trust — read this

Normal to worry about “giving ads to AI.” You don’t have to full-auto on day one.

Advise (read-only, recommended) — it reads the account and tells you daily what to turn off / scale — you click. Great if you’re newer to ads.

Execute (write, optional) — once you trust it, you can also text it (“raise this ad set”, “kill that ad”).

Coach Connect stays see-only either way. Write never required for checkoff. You own this.
